## 🌟 What is this Platform?

The **Ethical AI-Native Web Crawler** is not a simple scraper. It is a comprehensive **Full-Stack Reverse-Engineering & Web Intelligence Platform**. It combines:
1. **Ethical high-performance web crawling** strictly adhering to `robots.txt`, concurrency controls, politeness delays, and identifiable headers.
2. **Autonomous visual deconstruction** that takes full-page screenshots of complex web applications and reverse-engineers their design tokens, database schemas (ERD), API specifications, atomic frontend component hierarchies, and user journeys.
3. **Boardroom-grade executive PDF reporting** with crisp typography, vector wireframes, and exact page layouts.
4. **Automated change tracking & alerts** that monitor target websites for changes, filter DOM noise, and deliver diff webhooks.
5. **Local RAG & competitive intelligence** that runs on air-gapped local ChromaDB vector storage without data leakage.

## 💡 Why to Use

Traditional web scrapers produce unstructured blobs of HTML or messy text that require hours of human cleanup, break on dynamic JavaScript SPAs, get blocked by rate limits, and provide zero architectural insight.

**This platform changes that by providing:**
- **Zero-Guesswork Reverse-Engineering**: Inspect any competitor or benchmark application and receive an enterprise architectural specification including database schemas, REST contracts, and component trees.
- **Ethical & Unblockable Crawling**: Built-in adherence to web ethics ensures low ban rates, polite crawl delays, automatic sitemap traversal, and transparent User-Agent identification.
- **Dual-Engine Flexibility**: Lightning-fast `httpx` async crawler for static sites and headless Chromium `playwright` for JavaScript SPAs with auto-escalation.
- **Zero Data Loss & Strict Database Preservation**: Safe upsert mechanisms that cache crawled pages locally in ChromaDB without overwriting or truncating active data.

## 🔒 Why is this Required & Important?

1. **Compliance & Legal Safety**: Crawling without respecting `robots.txt`, without rate limiting, or with spoofed User-Agents exposes organizations to IP bans, CAPTCHA walls, and legal liability. This system guarantees compliance by default.
2. **Speed of Architecture Discovery**: Reverse-engineering an enterprise SaaS product traditionally takes weeks of manual UI inspection, network proxying, and documentation writing. This platform automates the entire process in under 60 seconds.
3. **Continuous Market & Competitor Awareness**: In rapidly evolving markets, pricing changes, product launches, and technological pivots happen without announcement. Automated scheduled diffing ensures your team is notified the instant a competitor updates their site.
4. **Air-Gapped Privacy**: Crawled knowledge, competitive vectors, and intelligence queries are stored locally on your machine, preventing proprietary research from leaking to third-party scrapers or cloud providers.

## ⏱️ When to Use vs. When Not to Use

### ✅ When to Use:
- **Product Architecture & Reverse-Engineering**: You need to understand how a SaaS application or website is structured, its underlying data models, its APIs, and its frontend layout.
- **Competitive Market Intelligence**: You need to compare multiple competitors side-by-side across features, pricing, audience, and technology stacks.
- **Website Change Monitoring**: You need automated recurring checks that alert via Slack or Discord whenever specific content, pricing, or terms change.
- **AI / LLM Data Pipelines**: You need clean, token-efficient GitHub-Flavored Markdown or Pydantic JSON schemas to train or prompt LLMs.
- **Offline Knowledge Retrieval**: You want to crawl full documentation sets and chat with them offline using local vector search (RAG).

### ❌ When NOT to Use:
- **Unauthorized Scraping of Private / Auth-Walled Data**: Do not use to bypass paywalls, circumvent CAPTCHAs, or violate Terms of Service on platforms that explicitly forbid crawling.
- **DDoS or High-Frequency Spamming**: Do not use to flood web servers. Always keep politeness delays and rate limits enabled.

## 🚀 Step-by-Step "How to Use" Guide

### Workflow 1: Reverse-Engineering a Web App into a Full Architecture Blueprint

1. **Launch the platform UI**:
   ```bash
   crawler serve --port 8000
   ```
2. Navigate to `http://localhost:8000` and click the **Visual Blueprint** tab.
3. Select an existing crawled domain (or trigger a crawl with screenshot capture enabled).
4. Click **Generate Visual Blueprint**:
   - The platform will analyze all screenshots across routes.
   - It automatically extracts the **real color palette** from image pixels (Primary, Accent, Background, Text).
   - It classifies the domain archetype (e.g. *Media & Studio Production*, *E-Commerce*, or *Enterprise SaaS*).
   - It synthesizes ERD data entities, REST API endpoint contracts, Atomic Design components, and user journeys.
5. Click **Download Executive PDF** to receive a publication-grade, boardroom-ready 6-page PDF.

---

### Workflow 2: Point-and-Click Interactive CSS Selector Picker

1. Open the dashboard at `http://localhost:8000`.
2. Enter the target URL and open the **Element Picker**:
   - The target website is loaded inside a secure sandbox iframe with dangerous external scripts and popups stripped.
3. Hover over any element on the page (e.g., article title, price tag, product description).
4. Click to select:
   - The tool calculates the most resilient, unique CSS selector (e.g. `div.pricing-card > span.amount`).
   - It highlights all matching elements on the live page.
5. Click **Copy Selector** to use it directly in your automated scraping jobs or RPA workflows.

---

### Workflow 3: Scheduled Crawling, Content Diff Engine & Webhook Alerts

1. In the dashboard, navigate to **Scheduled Crawls**:
2. Click **Create New Schedule**:
   - **Target URL**: `https://competitor.com/pricing`
   - **Cron Expression**: `0 9 * * 1` (Every Monday at 9:00 AM) or interval (e.g. `Every 6 Hours`)
   - **Diff Threshold**: `5%` change
   - **Webhook URL**: Enter your Slack or Discord incoming webhook URL.
3. Click **Save Schedule**:
   - The background scheduler automatically crawls the page at scheduled intervals.
   - If content changes exceed the threshold, a unified visual diff is generated and dispatched to your webhook immediately.

---

### Workflow 4: Competitive Intelligence & Technology Stack Profiling

1. In the **Knowledge & Analysis** section, select **Technology Profiler**:
2. Enter any URL or domain (e.g. `https://example.com`):
   - Scans HTML, scripts, metadata, headers, and cookies.
   - Outputs detected frameworks (React, Tailwind, Next.js), analytics engines (Google Analytics, PostHog), CMS (WordPress, Webflow, Shopify), and infrastructure (Cloudflare, AWS).
3. To compare multiple competitors:
   - Enter domains (e.g. `domainA.com, domainB.com`).
   - Click **Generate Competitive Matrix** to receive a side-by-side feature comparison table.

---

### Workflow 5: Local ChromaDB Vector Search & Offline RAG Q&A

1. All crawled pages are automatically stored in the local vector database with semantic embeddings.
2. In the CLI or Dashboard:
   ```bash
   # Semantic concept search:
   crawler search "enterprise pricing tiers and seat limits"

   # Ask natural language questions with source citations:
   crawler ask "What are the main differences between Plan A and Plan B?"
   ```
3. Responses are formulated strictly from your locally crawled pages, with exact source citations and zero external data leaks.

---

### Workflow 6: Design System Studio & Theme Customization

1. Click the palette icon in the top navigation bar to open the **Design System Studio**.
2. Switch between built-in themes:
   - **Obsidian Dark** (developer dark mode with emerald accents)
   - **Editorial Light** (high-contrast publishing mode)
   - **Cyberpunk Neon** (vibrant high-tech aesthetic)
   - **Nordic Minimalist** (cool-toned Scandinavian palette)
3. Customize typography pairings, border radiuses, and surface elevations. All preferences are preserved in local storage.
